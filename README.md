# The One-Line Laugh

One funny line for make.fun's "The One-Line Laugh" bounty ($5 in USDC, top 3 pay $2/$2/$1).

## The line

> I bought a standing desk to stop sitting all day, and now I sit all day on a taller chair.

90 characters, one sentence, one everyday truth: the standing desk was supposed to end sitting all day, and the twist at the end — a taller chair — defeats the whole point in the most literal way possible.

## How it works

`main.py` is a standard-library-only program (os + shutil). It copies the pre-written `entry.txt` into the directory named by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `out`), byte for byte. No third-party imports, no network, no writes into its own directory.

## Why this line, against the judge's criteria

1. Funny within 200 characters: one sentence, 90 characters, no setup wasted.
2. Surprise at the end: the punchline lands on the last three words — "a taller chair" — and it still makes sense, because a taller chair is exactly what a standing desk invites.
3. Everyday truth: standing desks are everywhere, and everyone who bought one knows the temptation to sit anyway.
4. Economical: the phrase "sit all day" repeats on purpose, so the twist echoes the setup in three words.
5. Fresh: the joke is not about the desk failing, it is about the buyer defeating it with the most literal workaround possible.
